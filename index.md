# MeetMi

**A real-time AI copilot for your meetings.** MeetMi listens to the meeting, transcribes it on your device, and helps you while the conversation is still happening.

[**Download on the Mac App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6806721128?pt=129380201&ct=website&mt=8)

The iPhone version is on its way. One subscription covers both.

[中文](zh/)

## What it does

- **Live advice.** While people are talking, MeetMi suggests what to ask, what to push back on, and what you may have missed.
- **Deeper analysis in the background.** It keeps a running picture of topics, decisions, open questions, and numbers, and updates it as the meeting goes on.
- **Minutes when you hang up.** A structured summary with decisions and action items, saved to a folder you choose.
- **Grounded in your own documents.** Point MeetMi at a workspace folder and it reads your notes, contracts, and briefs before it answers.
- **Live translation and transcript**, in English and Chinese.

## Private by design

- Speech recognition and translation run on your device. Raw audio is never saved or uploaded.
- No account and no sign-up. The only gate is the App Store subscription.
- Only after you consent does transcript text go to the AI service, and only through providers with zero data retention. You can use MeetMi with on-device transcription alone.
- Transcripts and minutes stay on your device and in the folder you pick.

## Pricing

One monthly subscription with a one-week free trial, billed through the App Store. The price for your region is shown in the app before you subscribe. Cancel any time in your Apple Account subscription settings.
